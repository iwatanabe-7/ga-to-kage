## Title
蛾と影 — Moth & Shadow

## Description（本文。ここから下を貼る）

**One creature freezes in your light. The other is drawn to it.**

It is 2:00 a.m. at *Tsukimi-sō*, a Japanese inn abandoned long ago. Five paper talismans (*ofuda*) are pasted up somewhere in its rooms. Tear all five down, then escape through the entrance you came in by.

You have one flashlight, and the batteries won't last.

### Two things live here

**THE SHADOW (影 / kage)**
It cannot move while your light is on it. Look away or switch off, and it comes closer.

**THE MOTH (蛾 / ga)**
She is drawn to your light, and the longer you shine it at her, the faster she comes. Switch off and she loses you. She can still hear you run.

Keeping the light on brings the moth. Turning it off frees the shadow. You have to keep choosing between them.

### Features
- A new inn layout every run
- Light is blocked by walls, so every corner you can't see is a place something can wait
- Sound generated in real time, with stereo positioning. **Play with headphones:** you can hear which side the wings and the crawling are coming from.
- Five fragments on the backs of the talismans tell what happened at the inn
- Three endings
- Fully playable in **English and Japanese** (switch on the title screen)
- About 5–15 minutes per run

### Tips
- When you hear wings, turn your light off and walk away quietly. Don't run near her.
- Before you switch off, know where the shadow is. When you can't see it, its eyes still glow faintly in the dark.
- In the dark, the faint red glow shows where an unclaimed talisman is.
